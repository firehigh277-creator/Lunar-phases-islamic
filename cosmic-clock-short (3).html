The Hijri calendar: main points

  • It is a lunar calendar of 12 months, each 29 or 30 days.
  • The year is about 10–11 days shorter than the solar year, so Ramadan moves through the seasons.
  • A month traditionally begins with sighting the young crescent. Some places also use calculations.
  • Four months are sacred (highlighted below).
  1. 1. MuharramAshura on the 10th
  2. 2. SafarNot a month of bad luck
  3. 3. Rabi' al-AwwalThird month
  4. 4. Rabi' al-ThaniFourth month
  5. 5. Jumada al-UlaFifth month
  6. 6. Jumada al-AkhirahSixth month
  7. 7. RajabSacred month
  8. 8. Sha'banMuch voluntary fasting
  9. 9. RamadanObligatory fasting
  10. 10. ShawwalEid al-Fitr; six fasts
  11. 11. Dhul-Qa'dahSacred month
  12. 12. Dhul-HijjahHajj, Arafah, Eid al-Adha

Key Qur'anic verses

وَقَدَّرَهُ مَنَازِلَ لِتَعْلَمُوا عَدَدَ السِّنِينَ وَالْحِسَابَ

The Moon was given phases so that people may know the count of years and calculation.

Yunus 10:5 ↗
يَسْأَلُونَكَ عَنِ الْأَهِلَّةِ ۖ قُلْ هِيَ مَوَاقِيتُ لِلنَّاسِ وَالْحَجِّ

The new crescents mark times for people and for Hajj.

Al-Baqarah 2:189 ↗
إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ عِندَ اللَّهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا

The number of months with Allah is twelve, and four of them are sacred.

At-Tawbah 9:36 ↗

Key Hadith

  • Begin and end the Ramadan fast by the crescent; if it is hidden, complete thirty days. Bukhari 1909 ↗
  • The sacred months are Dhul-Qa'dah, Dhul-Hijjah, Muharram and Rajab. Bukhari 4662 ↗
  • Fasting Ashura expiates the previous year's minor sins; fasting Arafah (for those not on Hajj) covers the previous and coming year. Three fasts each month are encouraged. Muslim 1162a ↗
  • Ramadan followed by six fasts in Shawwal is like fasting the whole year. Muslim 1164a ↗
  • Good deeds in the first ten days of Dhul-Hijjah are especially virtuous. Bukhari 969 ↗
